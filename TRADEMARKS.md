_Last updated: 19 October 2026_

Pathrule's code is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). That licence covers the
code. It does not cover the names, logos and other brand features that identify Pathrule: section 6
of the licence says so expressly. This policy explains how you may use them, so that anyone who sees
the Pathrule name can trust where the software behind it comes from.

## The marks

This policy covers, together the "Marks":

- the name **Pathrule**, and the product names that contain it, such as **Pathrule Cloud** and
  **PathruleBench**;
- the Pathrule logo, both the "P" symbol on its own and the symbol with the wordmark;
- any name, logo or domain that is confusingly similar to them.

The Marks belong to Sertan Helvacı, operating under the Pathrule brand ("we" in this policy).

## Uses that need no permission

You may, without asking us:

- **Refer to Pathrule** by name in articles, books, tutorials, talks, reviews, comparisons and
  criticism, as long as what you say is accurate.
- **Say that your work is compatible with Pathrule**, for example "for Pathrule", "works with
  Pathrule", "integrates with Pathrule" or "built on Pathrule", where that is true. Your own name must
  come first and stand apart: "Acme Sync for Pathrule", not "Pathrule Sync".
- **Redistribute Pathrule unmodified** under the name Pathrule, together with its LICENSE and NOTICE.
- **Link to Pathrule** with the unmodified logo, for example on a page that lists the tools you use.
- **Run a non-commercial community** about Pathrule, such as a meetup, a user group or a chat
  channel, as long as it is clear that we do not run it.

None of these uses may suggest that we sponsor, endorse, certify or partner with you.

## Uses that need written permission

Ask us before you:

- use a Mark, or anything confusingly similar, in the name or branding of a product, service,
  company, organisation, domain name, social media account or package, for example "Pathrule Pro",
  "PathRules" or "pathrule-plus";
- distribute a modified version of Pathrule under the Pathrule name or logo;
- offer a hosted, managed or commercial service that uses a Mark in its name or its marketing, or
  that presents itself as Pathrule Cloud or as an official Pathrule service;
- suggest a partnership, certification, sponsorship or endorsement by us;
- sell merchandise that carries a Mark;
- change the logo or combine it with another name or logo.

## Forks and modified versions

The Apache License lets you fork Pathrule and distribute your changes. A modified version must not be
distributed as Pathrule. If you distribute one:

- give it a name that is not confusingly similar to Pathrule;
- remove the Pathrule logo and branding from its interface, documentation and listings;
- do not publish it under the `@pathrule` npm scope, the `Pathrule` editor-extension publisher or the
  `pathrule` GitHub organisation, and do not ship it as the `pathrule` command or as the
  `Pathrule.pathrule-vscode` extension.

You may say, in plain text, that your project is "based on Pathrule" or "a fork of Pathrule", and you
must keep the LICENSE and NOTICE files the Apache License requires.

## Official channels

Official Pathrule software is published only from:

- the `@pathrule` scope on npm;
- the `Pathrule` publisher on the Visual Studio Code Marketplace and Open VSX;
- the [`pathrule`](https://github.com/pathrule) organisation on GitHub;
- [pathrule.io](https://www.pathrule.io).

Do not publish under these names, imitate them, or register names that could be mistaken for them.

## Using the logo

Where this policy or our permission allows you to use the logo:

- use the files from this repository unmodified, in white on a dark background or dark on a light one;
- keep its proportions and leave clear space around it;
- do not animate, distort, recolour or crop it, and do not combine it with other marks;
- do not make it more prominent than your own name or logo.
